Support

Contact Requva support

Email support.requva@gmail.com. A person reads every message.

How support works

  • Replies are written by hand and may take a few days. Requva does not send automatic emails today.
  • Include the email address of your account and what you were doing. Never send your password or recovery answer.

Common requests

  • I am locked out of my account. Password reset normally works with your recovery answer, so try that first.
  • I want my data deleted or exported
  • I want to report a listing. You can also use the Report button on any listing.
  • I have a question or idea
