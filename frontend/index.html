How it works

Three moves, no waiting room.

01

List it

Add your product and pass automatic checks. Valid listings go live at once unless a check flags them. Nobody queues for approval.

02

Get real eyes

People search, visit and leave voluntary feedback. Open a test and opted-in testers follow your instructions.

03

Read honest numbers

Views, clicks, tests and feedback are counted as measured. If something is not tracked, we say so instead of showing zero.

One account, three ways in

Be a founder, an explorer, a tester, or all three.

Tester

Our rules

What Requva will not do.

  • Invent reviews or testers.
  • Sell ranking or fake popularity.
  • Share tester emails with founders.
  • Claim a result it did not measure.
