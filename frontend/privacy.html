Privacy

Privacy notice

Plain language summary of what Requva stores and why. Last updated October 2026.

What we store

  • Account: your email address, a salted hash of your password, a recovery question you choose with a hash of its answer, and which capabilities (founder, product user, tester) you activated.
  • Listings: what founders submit about their products, including the website address.
  • Testing and feedback: your opt in, consent time, progress (opted in, started, feedback submitted) and the feedback text you write. Founders see feedback and participation counts, not your email address.
  • Notifications: alerts shown inside Requva about activity on your products or your feedback.
  • Usage counts: listing views and outbound clicks, stored with a daily rotating hash of network address and browser details so repeat clicks can be ignored. The raw address is not kept with these events.
  • Operational logs: an audit log of important actions and short lived rate limit records used to block abuse.

On your device

When you log in, a session token is stored in your browser storage and may also be set as a cookie. It lets you stay signed in. Logging out revokes it.

AI features

If a founder uses the optional AI tools, the product details and feedback needed for that request are sent to Google Gemini through Requva’s server. Do not put secrets in listings or feedback.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data or show ads.
  • We do not email you. Requva has no email delivery today, so account recovery uses your recovery answer, and alerts appear inside the app.
  • We do not share tester contact details with founders.

Your choices

You can withdraw feedback and tester participation from your dashboard. To ask for your account and data to be deleted, use the contact details provided by the site operator.
