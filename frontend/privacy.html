Privacy

Privacy notice

Plain language summary of what Requva stores and why. Last updated October 22, 2026.

What we store

  • Account: your email address, a salted hash of your password, a recovery question you choose with a hash of its answer, and which capabilities (founder, product user, tester) you activated.
  • Optional profile: a display name and a country or city, if you add them. They appear next to your feedback and replies with your role. Your email is never shown to other users.
  • Terms acceptance: which version of the Terms you accepted and when.
  • Listings: what founders submit about their products, including the website address.
  • Testing and feedback: your opt in, consent time, progress and the messages you write, including edits and deletions.
  • Notifications: alerts shown inside Requva about activity on your products or messages.
  • Usage counts: listing views, outbound clicks and share button clicks, stored with a daily rotating hash of network address and browser details so repeats can be ignored. The raw address is not kept with these events.
  • Operational logs: an audit log of important actions and short lived rate limit records used to block abuse.

On your device

When you log in, a session token is stored in your browser storage and may also be set as a cookie so you stay signed in. Logging out revokes it.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data or show ads.
  • We do not send emails from the platform. Requva has no email delivery today, so account recovery uses your recovery answer and alerts appear inside the app.
  • We do not share tester contact details with founders.

Your choices

You can edit or remove your display name and location in Account settings, edit or delete your own messages, and withdraw from tests. To ask for your account and data to be deleted, email support.requva@gmail.com.
